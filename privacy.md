# FatBoy Clips - Privacy Policy

FatBoy Clips is a personal tool used by its owner to publish gaming highlight videos to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

It only connects to accounts its owner explicitly authorizes through Google or TikTok sign-in. OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never shared or sold.

No data about other users is collected, stored, or processed. Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account or TikTok app settings.
